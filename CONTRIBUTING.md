# Helping with AI101

This course gets better when the people taking it fix what's wrong. You don't need to be an expert, and you don't need a GitHub account: a message in the BUILD room of our Telegram group (https://t.me/ainarchyx) is always enough.

## Ways to help

**Fix a subtitle in your language.** Our translations are made with AI, so native speakers will spot clumsy lines.
- On GitHub: open the lesson's `subtitles/` folder, open the `.srt` file for your language, click the pencil icon, fix the text and propose the change. Keep the numbers and the timing lines (`00:01:02,345 --> 00:01:05,678`) exactly as they are; change only the text.
- Without GitHub: post the line number, the old text and your fix in BUILD.

**Report a mistake.** A wrong date, a broken step, a claim that needs a better source. Open an issue (the *Issues* tab above) or post in BUILD. If you can, add a link to a source.

**Help someone in BUILD.** Answering a newcomer's question is the most useful thing anyone can do here. The course's last task is exactly that.

**Suggest something.** A word that needs explaining, a step that confused you, a tool we should show as an alternative. Post it in BUILD or open an issue.

## A few rules

- **No personal data.** Not yours, not anyone else's. No serial numbers, product keys, emails or phone numbers in issues or posts.
- **No promotion.** No affiliate links, referral codes or "buy this" recommendations. When a tool is mentioned, it's because it helps the lesson, and we show an alternative.
- **Sources for claims.** If you add or change a fact, say where it comes from.
- **Plain words.** Write for someone who started this course yesterday.
- **Be kind.** Everyone here was a beginner once.
